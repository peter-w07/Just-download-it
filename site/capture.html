Good to know

  • Private. The tab you share goes straight from your browser to this page, and nowhere else. The picture is made on your computer.
  • GIFs record up to a minute at 10 frames a second, up to 800 pixels wide. In Chrome and Edge, press Scroll from here and scroll with your mouse wheel over the picture.
  • Want the video or song itself, not a picture of it? That takes the free Just download it extension.
