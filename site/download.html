Paste a link. Just download it.

Paste a link and pick what to save. X videos and photos, Twitch clips, cover art and thumbnails download right here, in your browser, with nothing to install. For YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and full songs, the free extension opens with the link.

Shortcut: in the address bar, put justdownloadit.peterwild.pw/ in front of any link and press Enter.

Downloading this needs the free extension

Browsers only let a website fetch another site’s files when that site allows it, and most don’t. The Just download it extension can, inside your browser, with nothing uploaded.

Three ways to use it

  1. Paste a link here. X posts, Twitch clips, Apple Music covers and previews, and YouTube, TikTok and Spotify thumbnails download right on this page. With the extension installed, it opens with the link instead and gets everything, YouTube and TikTok videos and full songs included.
  2. Type it in front of a link. Add justdownloadit.peterwild.pw/ before any address, like justdownloadit.peterwild.pw/youtube.com/watch?v=…, and you land here with it filled in.
  3. Screenshot or GIF any tab. No install needed: share a tab with this site and save any part of it.
