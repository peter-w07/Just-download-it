Just download it can’t read the pages you visit yet. It needs to, to show its Download buttons, find the videos, songs and photos on a page, and look up the links you paste. Nothing you browse leaves your computer.

Albums, playlists and carousels can be saved as separate files, as one ZIP, or (for songs) as one mix.

Big videos, ZIPs and mixes are put together on your computer, and a progress bubble shows how it’s going. Long 4K videos, Twitch broadcasts and long playlists can take a while.

How to use it

  1. On YouTube, click Download next to Like. On YouTube Music, Spotify and Apple Music, it’s next to the play buttons. On Instagram and X, use the download icon in the post’s buttons (next to Share). On Twitch, it’s in the player controls. On TikTok, Facebook, Snapchat and Medal, point at a video and a download button appears in its corner.
  2. Got a link? Click the Just download it button in Chrome’s toolbar and paste it, or download from the tab you’re on. It can also save the page as a PNG or a GIF, and its gear opens this page.
  3. Anywhere else, right-click a photo or video and pick Just download media. To save part of a page, pick Just download web page: as a PNG image, or as a GIF you record while you scroll. Settings… in the same menu opens this page.
  4. Choose a quality. The best one is already selected, so Enter saves it.
  5. On an album or playlist, Download all saves every song as its own file. Click the ▸ arrow next to it for Download as ZIP (one file with everything) or Combine into one mix (one long track that fades from song to song).

Free, and it all happens on your computer. Settings follow your Chrome profile when sync is on.
